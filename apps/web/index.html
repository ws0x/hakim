Built for Serious Readers & Thinkers

Everything you need to turn raw highlights into lifelong personal intelligence.

Autonomous Background Sync

Runs silently in the background every 6 hours and upon opening Chrome. Highlights taken on your Kindle e-reader are waiting in Notion before you even open your computer.

Two-Way Synced Reading OS

Establishes true reciprocal backlinks between your Books database and Highlights database. Open any book in Notion to see all of its quotes automatically displayed.

Custom 12+ Field Mapping

Already have a personal Notion database schema? Map custom column names for titles, authors, quotes, user notes, chapters, locations, and personal interpretations.

100% Private & Local-First

Zero cloud intermediaries. Your Amazon cookies never leave your browser context, and your reading highlights stream directly between Kindle Cloud and Notion API.

2-Second Cached Checkpointing

Intelligent incremental sync skips unchanged books and pagination, turning repeated syncs from minutes into a lightning-fast 2-second background check.

Semantic Color Taxonomy

Transform raw yellow and pink colors into an active knowledge management ontology: Key Insights, Quotes & Facts, Critical Action Items, and Memorable Concepts.

Install in 60 Seconds

Zero CLI, zero terminal commands, zero subscriptions.

1

Download Extension Bundle

Download the latest pre-built extension release package (.zip) from GitHub.

Download hakim-v1.2.1.zip
2

Load Unpacked in Chrome

Unzip the folder, open chrome://extensions in your browser, toggle Developer Mode (top-right), and click Load unpacked selecting the apps/extension folder.

3

Connect Notion & Sync

Click the Hakim icon in your toolbar, paste your Notion Secret and Page URL, then click Sync. Hakim auto-provisions your Notion databases and handles background syncs!

Terminal (Developer Setup) 
# 1. Clone the repository
git clone https://github.com/hakim-reading/hakim.git
cd hakim

# 2. Install dependencies & build extension
pnpm install

# 3. Load in browser
# Open chrome://extensions -> Load Unpacked -> Select /apps/extension/dist
